# Magwell - Faces of War

Adds the Faces of War magazines, clips and belts to the shared CBA wells of their real weapon (K98k, Lee-Enfield, M1903, Type 99, Bren, FG 42, BAR, M1919, MG 34 / 42, Type 99 LMG, G43, M1 Carbine, M1 Garand, StG 44, Thompson, M3, Reising, MP 40, Sten, Type 100, Owen, MAB 38, PIAT, Bazooka, 12 gauge) and lets each weapon accept its well. The G43 also takes K98k stripper clips, as the real rifle does.

Left out from the source: the M7 / Type 2 rifle grenade launcher muzzles (nested muzzle classes, not ported).

Based on [Weapons-Balance-FOW](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-FOW) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
