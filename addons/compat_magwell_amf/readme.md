# Magwell - AMF

Adds the AMF (Arma Mod France) magazines to the shared CBA / BI wells: FAMAS F1 25-round, Minimi 150-round boxes, HK417 20-round, MAG 58 / AA-NF1 belts and MP5 30-round.

Weapons: the FAMAS F1 takes FAMAS magazines; the FAMAS G2, HK416 (614) and SCAR-L take STANAG magazines only (they inherit the FAMAS F1 / SCAR-H wells otherwise); the HK417 (714) and SCAR-H take their own pattern; the MAG 58 / AA-NF1, Minimi, MP5 and Glock 17 take the shared belts / magazines.

Left out from the source: STANAG wells on the SIG 552 (the SG 550 family uses its own magazines).

Based on [Weapons-Balance-AMF](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-AMF) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2022 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
