# Magwell - Project Blufor

Adds the Project Blufor magazines to the shared CBA wells (5.56 AK, FAMAS, 7.62x51 belts, MG3 belts).

Weapons: the C7, K2 / K2C1, MSBS Grot, SCAR-L and CZ BREN 2 (5.56) take STANAG magazines, the C9 STANAG and belts, the AR-M1 and RPK 5.56 the 5.56 AK well, the RK 62 / RK 62 M2 and Beryl wz. 96 variants the matching AK wells, the G3, G36, HK417, MG3, Minimi 7.62 and FAMAS F1 their own pattern.

Left out from the source: AK wells on the CZ BREN 2 7.62x39 (it takes its own magazines), M14 wells on the MPT-76, the SR-25 well on the RAHE, the 7.62x54R wells and name of the "Negev" (the real NG-7 is 7.62x51) and the STANAG C-Mag on the G36.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-PB](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-PB) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
