# Magwell - RHS USAF (Weapons Balance)

Adds RHS USAF magazines to the shared CBA / BI wells: the 200-round tracer SAW box (`CBA_556x45_MINIMI`, `M249_556x45`), the M240 / M60 7.62x51 belts (`CBA_762x51_LINKS` 12, `M240_762x51` 2), the SR-25 / Mk 11 magazines (`CBA_762x51_SR25`, 4) and the FIM-92 missile (`CBA_FIM`). The SR-25 accepts `CBA_762x51_SR25` and the FIM-92 launcher accepts `CBA_FIM`.

Complements `compat_magwell_rhs` (STANAG wells). Left out from the source: the M995 soft pouches and the XM2010 magazine in the AI AWM well (AICS and AWM magazines are not interchangeable).

Based on [Weapons-Balance-RHS](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-RHS) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
