# Magwell - JCA Gen 4

Adds the JCA PMAG 5.56x45 magazines to the shared `Stanag_556x45_Big` / `CBA_556x45_STANAG` magazine wells and makes the M4A1 and M4A4 accept the shared 5.56x45 STANAG wells.

Adds the JCA SR PMAG / SMAG 7.62x51 magazines to the CBA `CBA_762x51_AR10` well and makes the SR10 and SR25 use it. They do not accept HK417, SCAR, FAL, G3 or M14 magazines.
