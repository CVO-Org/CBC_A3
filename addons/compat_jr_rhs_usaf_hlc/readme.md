# Joint Rails - RHS USAF on HLC

Adds RHS foregrips (17) and Harris bipods to the NIArms (HLC) underbarrel slots `nia_rifle_grips_slot` and `nia_rifle_bipodsgrips_slot`. Grips from RHS AFRF are listed too and only show up when it is loaded.

The source's own `WB_rifle_grips_slot` and its slot rename are left out.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-RHS](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-RHS) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Attachment lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
