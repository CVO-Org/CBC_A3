# Magwell - GEIST

Adds the GEIST magazines and clips to the shared CBA wells of their real weapon (Webley .38/200, MAS-36, Mosin clips, LS/26, PA 1935A, wz. 1928, Modèle 1892, FN GP35, Suomi KP/-31 drums and sticks, Lahti L-35). The KP/-31 accepts the Suomi drum and stick wells.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-GEIST](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-GEIST) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
