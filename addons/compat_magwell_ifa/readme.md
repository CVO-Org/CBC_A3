# Magwell - IFA3

Adds the IFA3 magazines, clips and belts to the shared CBA wells of their real weapon (35 wells: .30-06, .303, 7.62x54R, 7.92x57, 7.92x33, 9x19, .45 ACP, 7.62x25, PTRD, Bazooka, PIAT, ...) and lets each weapon accept its well. Stripper-clip loaders accept their clips as in reality: G41 / G43 K98k clips, SVT-40 Mosin clips, De Lisle M1911 magazines.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-IFA](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-IFA) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2022 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
