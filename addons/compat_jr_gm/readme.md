# Joint Rails - Global Mobilization

Adds GM attachments to the shared slots: NSPU / PKA / ZFK 4x25 / ZLN-1K on the Warsaw Pact side rails, ZFK and PSO-1 on the SVD rail, GM rail optics and lights on `asdg_OpticRail1913`, PBS-1 / PBS-4 / ATEC suppressors and the SVD bayonet on the matching muzzle slots.

Left out from the source: the PGO-7V (an RPG-7 sight) on the AK rails, the G3 bayonet on the generic 5.56 slot, the MP5 Navy / KAC / TGP-P suppressors on the generic 9 mm slot and the GM muzzle slot inheritance changes.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-GM](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-GM) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Attachment lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
