# Magwell - ACE CBA

Adds ACE ammunition variants to the shared CBA wells: the 6-round 12 gauge pellet loads (`CBA_12g_6rnds`) and the dim-tracer MX / Katiba magazines (`CBA_65x39_MX`, `CBA_65x39_MX_XL`, `CBA_65x39_Katiba`).

Complements `compat_magwell_ace` (STANAG wells). Left out from the source: the 8-round 12 gauge shells (ACE has no such magazines), the ACE CSW 100-round .50 BMG boxes (`CBA_50BMG_100rnds` is not a CBA well), 20-round .300 Win Mag magazines in the AI / AICS wells (real AI .300 magazines hold 5), cased 6.5 Creedmoor / 6.5x47 magazines in the caseless MX / MSBS wells, and the fictional MAR-10 magazines.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-ACE](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-ACE) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Checked against the ACE3 and CBA configs, not tested in game.
