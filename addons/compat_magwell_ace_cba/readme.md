# Magwell - ACE CBA

Adds ACE ammunition variants to the shared CBA wells: 6- and 8-round 12 gauge shell loads (`CBA_12g_6rnds`, `CBA_12g_8rnds`), the dim-tracer MX / Katiba magazines (`CBA_65x39_MX`, `CBA_65x39_MX_XL`, `CBA_65x39_Katiba`) and the ACE CSW 100-round .50 BMG belt boxes (`CBA_50BMG_100rnds`).

Complements `compat_magwell_ace` (STANAG wells). Left out from the source: 20-round .300 Win Mag magazines in the AI / AICS wells (real AI .300 magazines hold 5), cased 6.5 Creedmoor / 6.5x47 magazines in the caseless MX / MSBS wells, and the fictional MAR-10 magazines.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-ACE](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-ACE) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
