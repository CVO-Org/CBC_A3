# Names - ACE realistic names

On top of ACE realistic names: the vanilla LIM-85 is named after the rifle it models, the "FN Minimi Mk3", and the Protector after the "HK MP5K-PDW".

Left out from the source: the AKMN, AK-15 (GP-25) and RPKM renames, which would leave the matching vanilla rifles with different names.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-ACE](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-ACE) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Display names only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
