# Joint Rails - ACE on 3CB

Adds the ACE 5.56 flash suppressor to the 3CB Factions HK33 muzzle slot.

Left out from the source: the 7.62 flash suppressor on the G3 / M14 slots (like `compat_jr_hlc`, those slots only get suppressors that fit the 3CB models) and the `asdg_MuzzleSlot_900_3CB` entry (no mod defines that slot).

Based on [Weapons-Balance-ACE](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-ACE) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Attachment lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
