# Magwell - Project Infinite

Adds the Project Infinite magazines to the shared CBA wells: 12 gauge loads, .300 Win Mag (AICS), .303 (Lee-Enfield) and the RK 95 TP 7.62x39 magazine (AK).

Weapons: the Falkor takes AICS magazines, the No.4 Mk.I .303 clips, the Model 97 and SPR 220 12 gauge shells, the RK 95 TP AK / RPK magazines.

Left out from the source: the AI .338 / MAR-10 wells for the TRG-42 (Sako TRG magazines differ), the AI AWM well for the Falkor and the fictional MAR-10 well.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-PI](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-PI) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
