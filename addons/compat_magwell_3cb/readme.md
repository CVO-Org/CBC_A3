# Magwell - 3CB Factions

Adds the 3CB Factions magazines (all colour / tracer variants) to the shared CBA / BI wells of their real pattern: AK-74 / AK-47 / RK 62 / RPK, M1903, FAMAS F1, P90, Stgw 57 and its SIG AMT, PPSh drum, RPD, SKS, FAL / L1A1 (incl. the L4 Bren 30-round, which fits the L1A1), M60 and MG3 belts, Uk vz. 59 belts, CZ 75, Hi-Power, MP5 9 mm / 10 mm, Sten, AUG 9 mm, Uzi, M16 and SVD.

Weapons: the Saiga 12K, FAMAS F1, M60 (incl. mounted), MG3, Sterling, Uk vz. 59, Hi-Power and CZ 75 accept the matching wells.

Left out from the source: the white-tracer magazines Weapons Balance adds itself, the AR-18 magazines in the STANAG well (the AR-18 takes its own magazine), FAL magazines in the L4 Bren (only the other way round fits) and the MX wells on the fictional caseless G36 MLI(C).

Based on [Weapons-Balance-3CBFactions](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-3CBFactions) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
