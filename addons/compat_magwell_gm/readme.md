# Magwell - Global Mobilization

Adds the Global Mobilization magazines to the shared CBA / BI wells of their real weapon (AK-47 / AK-74 / RPK, G3, HK33, G36, SG 550, STANAG, MG3 and PK belts, SVD, PM, PM-63, P1, MP5, MP2, HK512, 40 mm, 26.5 mm flares, Carl Gustaf, RPG-7, Strela) and lets each weapon accept its well.

Left out from the source: the Pallad in the M203 well (it fires 40x47 mm, not 40x46 mm NATO) and the FIM-43 Redeye in the FIM-92 well.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-GM](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-GM) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
