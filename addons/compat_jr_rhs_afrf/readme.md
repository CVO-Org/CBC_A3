# Joint Rails - RHS AFRF

Adds RHS AFRF attachments to the shared vanilla and CBA Joint Rails slots, so they fit other mods' weapons:

- Picatinny optics (5) and lasers (3) on `CowsSlot_Rail` / `PointerSlot_Rail`.
- PSO-1 scopes (20, including their mode-switch variants) on the Warsaw Pact side rail `asdg_OpticSideRail_AK`.
- 5.45x39 AK muzzle devices (5) on `asdg_MuzzleSlot_545R` / `MuzzleSlot_545R`.
- The AKM slant compensator on `asdg_MuzzleSlot_762R` / `MuzzleSlot_762R`.

Only attachments that physically fit are listed. Left out from the source's 7.62x39 list: the AK-74 / AK-100 brakes and Zenitco DTK devices (24x1.5 thread, not the AKM's 14x1 LH), the PGS-64 (5.45) and the AK-5.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-RHS](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-RHS) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Attachment lists only, no balance changes.

Class names checked against the RHS AFRF 0.5.6 configs.
