# Magwell - CSLA

Adds the CSLA Iron Curtain magazines to the shared CBA / BI wells of their real weapon: Sa vz. 58, Sa vz. 61, vz. 52, vz. 82, UK vz. 59 belts, OP-63 (SVD), 9K32 Strela, PG-7M110 (RPG-7), 26.5 mm flares, and the US85 STANAG, M249, M60 belts, M1911, M9, MP5, 12 gauge, 40 mm M203, MAAWS, SMAW and FIM-92.

Weapons: the OP-63, M16, M14 / M21, M249, M60, FIM-92, SMAW and 9K32 accept the matching wells.

Left out from the source: CSLA's single 20-round 7.62x51 magazine in both the FAL and M14 wells (the two patterns differ), the flare well on the base grenade launcher, and the unidentified `UGL_40x36` well.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-CSLA](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-CSLA) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
