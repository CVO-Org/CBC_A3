# BettIR - ACE

Registers the ACE night vision goggles (21 binocular / wide / generation variants and the monocular) with BettIR, so BettIR's IR illumination shows through them. Also removes the ACE green laser, which is visible light only, from BettIR's IR attachments, and registers the ACE IR green laser instead.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-ACE](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-ACE) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. BettIR compatibility only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
