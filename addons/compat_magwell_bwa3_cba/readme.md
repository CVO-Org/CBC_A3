# Magwell - BWA3 CBA

Adds BWMod magazines to the shared CBA / BI wells of their real pattern: P12 (HK USP .45), P8 (HK USP 9 mm), MP7, G82 (Barrett M82 / M107), MG4 200-round boxes (Minimi / M249), MG3 / MG5 7.62x51 belts, Carl Gustav rounds, the Fliegerfaust (FIM-92) missile and the P2A1 26.5 mm flares. Each of those weapons accepts its CBA well, and the G28 also the long HK417 wells.

Complements `compat_magwell_bwa3` (G36 / HK417 / STANAG). Left out from the source: the G36 well on the G38 (an HK416, see `compat_magwell_bwa3`), the 1200-round vehicle box in the infantry MG wells, and the G29 (Haenel RS9) in the AI .338 well, which could not be confirmed.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-BW](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-BW) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
