# Magwell - Unsung

Adds the Unsung magazines, clips and belts to the shared CBA / BI wells of their real weapon (58 wells, from the AK, M14, M16, FAL and SKS to the Owen, Madsen M-50, MAT-49, MAS-49 and K-50M) and lets each weapon accept its well. Stripper-clip loaders accept their clips as in reality (SVT-40 Mosin clips).

Left out from the source: PPSh magazines in the PPS-43 (different magazine), STANAG wells on the Stoner 63 (its own magazine), the F1 SMG magazine in the Sten well and the unidentified `UGL_40x36` well.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-Unsung](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-Unsung) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
