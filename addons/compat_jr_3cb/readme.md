# Joint Rails - 3CB Factions

Adds the 3CB SUIT (L1A1 scope) to the CBA L1A1 optic rail `asdg_OpticRailL1A1`.

Left out from the source: the 3CB M14 / G3 / HK33 / MP5 suppressors on the generic 5.56 / 7.62 / 9 mm slots (each is made for its own rifle), the MP5/10 suppressor on the .45 slot (10 mm) and the `asdg_MuzzleSlot_900_3CB` entry (no mod defines that slot).

Based on [Weapons-Balance-3CBFactions](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-3CBFactions) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Attachment lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
