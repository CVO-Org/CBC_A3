# Joint Rails - Vanilla on 3CB

Adds the vanilla 5.56 suppressors to the 3CB Factions HK33 muzzle slot and the 7.62 suppressors to the G3 / M14 muzzle slots. The slot parents follow `compat_jr_hlc`. The source's `asdg_MuzzleSlot_900_3CB` entry is left out (no mod defines that slot).

Based on [Weapons-Balance-Vanilla](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-Vanilla) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Attachment lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
