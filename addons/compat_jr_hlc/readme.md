# Joint Rails - HLC

The `UK3CB_*` slot classes are not HLC's: the prefix is UK3CB (UK 3 Commando Brigade), and stock HLC weapons only use them where UK3CB or Weapons Balance - NIArms assigns them. This makes HLC attachments fit weapons that use those slots.

Adds HLC (NIArms) muzzle devices to the CBA Joint Rails slots `UK3CB_MuzzleSlot_556_HK33`, `UK3CB_MuzzleSlot_762_G3` and `UK3CB_MuzzleSlot_762_M14`.

HLC optics and the Agenda Six are not added, because 3CB Factions ships its own copies of them.

Only items that fit the slot's calibre are listed. The source's 5.56 suppressor on 7.62 slots, 10mm suppressor on .45, MAG58 brake, 300 BLK and SCAR-H muzzle devices and KX3 comp on 7.62 are left out.

Attachment lists only, no weapon changes.
