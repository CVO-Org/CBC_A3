# Joint Rails - 3CB BAF

Adds 3CB BAF attachments to the shared slots: LLM lights / lasers on `PointerSlot_Rail`, Picatinny optics (47: RKSL, ACOG, Specter, Kite / MaxiKite, S&B) on `CowsSlot_Rail`, the SUIT on the L1A1 rail `asdg_OpticRailL1A1`, and the SureFire flash hider on the 5.56 muzzle slots.

Left out from the source: SUSAT and SUIT on Picatinny rails (they need their own mounts), the blank firing adapters, L85 suppressor and bayonet, L115A3 .338 suppressor and L91A1 suppressor on generic slots, the BAF items on the 3CB Factions HK33 / G3 / M14 slots, and the inherit-everything changes to the BAF muzzle slots.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-3CBBAF](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-3CBBAF) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Attachment lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
