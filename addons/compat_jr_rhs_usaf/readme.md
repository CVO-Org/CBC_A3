# Joint Rails - RHS USAF

Adds RHS USAF attachments to the shared vanilla and CBA Joint Rails slots, so they fit other mods' weapons:

- Picatinny optics (55), lasers / lights (18) and Harris bipods on `CowsSlot_Rail`, `PointerSlot_Rail`, `UnderBarrelSlot_rail`.
- M8541 and Leupold Mk 4 scopes on the long rail `asdg_OpticRail1913_long`.
- The SilencerCo Omega 9K on `asdg_MuzzleSlot_9MM_SMG`.
- Harris bipods on the RHS western rifle / MG gripod slots and the M14 sling-swivel slot, plus the RHS MG grip on the MG gripod slot.

Left out from the source's generic 7.62x51 slots: the M14 DCQD suppressor, M14 flash suppressor and L1A1 muzzle, which only fit their own rifles.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-RHS](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-RHS) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Attachment lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
