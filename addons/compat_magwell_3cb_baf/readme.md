# Magwell - 3CB BAF

Adds the 3CB BAF magazines to the shared CBA / BI wells of their real pattern: L128A1 12 gauge, L115A3 .338 (AI), L123 UGL 40 mm (M203), L111A1 / L135A1 .50 BMG, L110 Minimi boxes, L85 STANAGs, L7 GPMG belts, L129A1 (SR-25 pattern), L91A1 MP5, L105 / L106 (P226), L107 / L117 (P228 / P229) and L131A1 (Glock 17).

Weapons: the SA80 family, L119, L110 (STANAG and belts), L7A2, L1A1 (metric FAL magazines fit the inch-pattern SLR), L128A1, L129A1, L135A1, L91A1, the SIG pistols, L131A1 and the vehicle L7 / L111 / L112 accept the matching wells.

Left out from the source: the L1A1 inch-pattern magazines in the metric FAL well (they do not fit metric FALs), the L129A1 magazine in the M14 well and the AR-10 well on the L129A1 (both other patterns), the 800-round vehicle belts in infantry wells, the unidentified `UGL_40x36` well, and the replaced `magazines[]` lists.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-3CBBAF](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-3CBBAF) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
