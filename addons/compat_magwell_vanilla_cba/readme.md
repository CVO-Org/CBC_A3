# Magwell - Vanilla CBA

Adds vanilla magazines to the shared CBA / BI wells where the real-world counterpart fits: the sand and green 150-round 5.56 drums, the hex / green hex CAR-95 100-round drums, AK-12 7.62x39 magazines and 75-round drums, the Mk14 10-round (M14 pattern), the Carl Gustaf HEAT55, 7.62x51 and 7.62x54R belt boxes, and 12 gauge shells.

Weapons: the Zafir takes `PK_762x54R` (RHS PK belts), and the Kozlice and MSBS underbarrel shotgun take CBA 12 gauge shells. The source's MP-443 well on the Rook-40 is left out: other mods' pistols are built on the Rook-40 (e.g. the 3CB CZ 75) and would inherit it.

Left out because CBA or the base game already has them: the sand STANAGs (`CBA_556x45_STANAG`, `STANAG_556x45`), the plain CAR-95 100-round drums, and `M249_556x45` on the LIM-85 (CBA gives it `CBA_556x45_MINIMI`, which RHS fills with the same boxes). Left out on calibre: `CBA_9x18_PM` on the PM, which fires 9x21.

Also left out from the source: the generic `20Rnd_762x51_Mag` in the FAL / G3 / HK417 / M14 / SCAR / SR-25 wells (those magazine patterns do not interchange), the Mk14 magazine in the G3 well, 9x21 magazines in the 9x18 / 9x19 wells, the fictional MAR-10 and Rahim magazines in the AI / SVD wells, and the AK-15 weapons Weapons Balance adds.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-Vanilla](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-Vanilla) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Magazine lists only, no balance changes.

Checked against the game and mod configs, not tested in game.
