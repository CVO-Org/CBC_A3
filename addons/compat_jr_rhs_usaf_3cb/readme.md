# Joint Rails - RHS USAF on 3CB

Adds RHS suppressors to the 3CB Factions muzzle slots: 5.56 suppressors and SureFire devices on `UK3CB_MuzzleSlot_556_HK33`, the AAC 762-SD / SDN-6 and the GREF SDN-6 on `UK3CB_MuzzleSlot_762_G3`, plus the M14 DCQD suppressor and M14 flash suppressor on `UK3CB_MuzzleSlot_762_M14`. Items from RHS GREF / SAF only show up when those are loaded.

Like `compat_jr_hlc`, the G3 slot only gets suppressors. Left out from the source: the SCAR-H suppressor, and the L1A1 muzzle and M14 devices on the G3.

Based on [Weapons-Balance-RHS](https://github.com/johnb432/Weapons-Balance-RHS) by johnb432, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2021 johnb432. Attachment lists only, no balance changes.

Not verified against the mod's configs and not tested in game.
